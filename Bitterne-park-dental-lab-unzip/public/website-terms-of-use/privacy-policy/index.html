Privacy notice

How information is used when you visit this website or contact us.

Who to contact

This website is for Bitterne Park Dental Laboratory. For privacy questions, email lbiffen@gmail.com or call 023 8067 1836.

Browsing this website

The website is hosted using Cloudflare. Hosting services may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect the website. This site does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or a patient portal.

Contacting us

When you telephone or email us, we receive the details you choose to provide. We use those details to respond to your enquiry and manage the arrangements you request. The website itself does not collect or store enquiry form submissions.

Email and clinical information

Email links open your email application. Please keep an initial enquiry to your name, contact details and a brief description of the service you need. Contact us to agree an appropriate way to share sensitive information.

Further privacy information

Please contact us for information about how your correspondence and any patient records are handled, including retention arrangements and requests relating to your personal information. This page describes this website; it is not a complete patient-record privacy notice.
